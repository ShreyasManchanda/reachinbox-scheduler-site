ReachInbox Scheduler

ReachInbox Scheduler is a full-stack email job scheduler built as a hiring assignment. Sign in with Google, upload or type a list of recipients, choose a start time, a delay between emails and an hourly limit, and the service schedules and sends each email at the right time.

What it does

  • Schedules one delayed job per email and keeps them across restarts, without duplicates.
  • Enforces per-sender hourly limits and a minimum gap between sends.
  • Shows Scheduled and Sent emails in a dashboard, with search.
  • Can post a Slack message when a sender reaches its hourly limit.

Sign-in

The app uses Google Sign-In and requests only your basic profile: name, email address and profile picture.
