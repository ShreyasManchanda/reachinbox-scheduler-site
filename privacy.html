Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy applies to ReachInbox Scheduler ("the app"), an email scheduling project built as a hiring assignment.

What we collect

  • Google account basics, when you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. The app requests only the openid, email and profile scopes.
  • Content you create in the app: email subjects and bodies, recipient addresses you upload or type, and scheduling settings.
  • Slack connection (optional): if you connect Slack, the workspace name, channel and the incoming-webhook URL Slack provides. The webhook URL is stored encrypted.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your identity in the app, schedule and send the emails you create, and send limit notifications to the Slack channel you chose. We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except that emails you schedule are handed to the configured SMTP provider to be sent, and Slack messages go to the workspace you connected.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in the app's database and queue. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing the address below.
